Grandma's Chili

Home

Description

This chili recipe will remind you of grandma's chili back home in Reno.

Ingredients

Large Pot

  • 2 pounds lean ground beef
  • 1 onion diced
  • 1 jalapeño seeded and finely diced
  • 4 cloves garlic minced
  • 2 ½ tablespoons chili powder divided, or to taste
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1 green bell pepper seeded and diced
  • 14.5 ounces crushed tomatoes 1 can
  • 19 ounces canned red kidney beans drained and rinsed
  • 14.5 ounces canned diced tomatoes with juices
  • 1 ½ cups beef broth
  • 1 cup beer
  • 1 tablespoon tomato paste
  • 1 tablespoon brown sugar optional
  • salt and black pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Heat a large pot over medium-high heat. Add the ground beef and cook until browned, breaking it up as you go.
  2. Add the onion, jalapeño, and garlic to the pot. Cook for 5 minutes.
  3. Add 2 tablespoons of the chili powder, cumin, and green pepper to the pot. Stir to combine.
  4. Add the crushed tomatoes, kidney beans, diced tomatoes, beef broth, beer, tomato paste, and brown sugar to the pot. Stir until well mixed.
  5. Season the chili with the remaining ½ tablespoon of chili powder, salt, and pepper.
